# Product Vision

## Product statement

Flash ERP should become a connected retail operating system for multi-store retailers that need resilient store execution, central enterprise control, and dependable two-way synchronization.

## What "iVend-like" means here

Using public retail product positioning as the benchmark, Flash ERP should eventually support:

- fixed POS and desktop store POS
- mobile-assisted selling and store-floor operations
- shared catalog, pricing, and inventory visibility
- centralized control with store autonomy during outages
- promotions, loyalty, and customer history
- omnichannel fulfillment and integration flows
- analytics, monitoring, and enterprise-grade auditability

## Primary personas

- cashier
- store supervisor
- store manager
- merchandising manager
- inventory controller
- finance and operations lead
- enterprise administrator
- sync and support operator

## Primary business outcomes

- stores continue trading when connectivity is weak or absent
- HQ controls master data without blocking store execution
- inventory movement remains auditable end to end
- sales, shifts, and payments replicate upstream reliably
- store and enterprise users work in consistent operational workspaces

## Phase 1 product boundary

Phase 1 should prove the platform foundation:

- enterprise master data baseline
- store desktop offline shell
- local queue and sync posture
- catalog
- inventory locations and ledger foundation
- sales, returns, and shifts
- sync monitoring baseline

Phase 1 should not yet include:

- full loyalty engine
- omnichannel orchestration
- ERP connector library
- advanced promotion stacking
- enterprise reporting warehouse
